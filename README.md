## Sessions

### Hidden in Plain Sight: Data Aggregation Risks Across the Digital Engineering Landscape

**Thursday, May 21 · 10:30 AM – 11:20 AM PDT · Nevada 1/2**

| | |
|---|---|
| Presenter | **Patrick Rose** — ESS AEGIS TECHREP, Systems Engineer Principal, BAE Systems · [patrick.rose2@baesystems.us](mailto:patrick.rose2@baesystems.us) |
| Co-Presenter | **David Fields** — Co-Founder / CTO, Enola Technologies · [david.fields@enolatech.com](mailto:david.fields@enolatech.com) |

The aggregation of data poses a distinct challenge to sensitive data protection: seemingly innocuous data can become sensitive when considered in relation to other data points. The issue is particularly pronounced in digital tools, which rely on querying and relation-based architectures. As these tools continue to evolve, the rules governing data protection have to evolve with them.

This session walks through concrete case studies in CATIA Magic / MagicDraw to help engineers identify where protected data hides in their tools, and makes the case for adaptive data-protection strategies in modern digital engineering environments.

→ Folder: [`hidden-in-plain-sight/`](./hidden-in-plain-sight/)

### MOSA Implementation: Standards-Based Interface Definition and Integration

**Wednesday, May 20 · 12:30 PM – 1:30 PM PDT · Nevada 12**

| | |
|---|---|
| Presenter | **Neil Patel** — Co-Founder / CEO, Enola Technologies · [neil.patel@enolatech.com](mailto:neil.patel@enolatech.com) |
| Co-Presenter | **Dr. Sarah Rudder** — Principal Systems Engineer, Enola Technologies · [sarah.rudder@enolatech.com](mailto:sarah.rudder@enolatech.com) |

CATIA MOSA supports modular open system architecture (MOSA) goals with the ability to create domain-specific languages via profiles for standards such as GCIA, DAML, and FACE. A modular system has precisely defined interfaces, which enables independent suppliers to provide improved capabilities, accelerates the delivery of advanced technologies, and drives down integration risk and lifecycle cost. Because MOSA puts interfaces first, it also supports infrastructures that integrate with legacy components.

This session gives the audience a guided look at the tool's MOSA capabilities and how they support mission requirements.

→ Folder: [`mosa-implementation/`](./mosa-implementation/)

## About Enola Technologies

Enola Technologies is a digital engineering and Model-Based Systems Engineering (MBSE) consultancy serving the U.S. Department of Defense, defense primes, and commercial systems organizations.

We help engineering teams move from document-centric to model-centric workflows — and we make sure the tooling, training, and automation actually hold up at program scale.

## What we do

### MBSE Training
From your team's first SysML diagram to advanced profile, DSL, simulation, and analysis topics. We train engineers, architects, and program leadership — not just on the *what* of SysML, but on the *how* of using it to drive program decisions.

### Tool Automation & Customization
MagicDraw / CATIA Magic API work, plugins, macros, scripts, and Velocity Template Language reporting. If your team is doing it by hand and shouldn't be, we can probably automate it.

### Profile & DSL Development
Domain-specific languages and stereotypes that capture your domain's vocabulary inside your tools — not buried in a wiki. Designed to evolve with the program, not freeze on day one.

### Enterprise MBSE Deployment
Pilot to program-wide adoption — tool selection, Teamwork Cloud setup, configuration management, governance, and the training pipeline that keeps the rollout sticking.

### MOSA & Interface Engineering
Profile-driven implementation of modular open system architecture standards (FACE, GCIA, DAML, and more), plus the interface definitions that make modular components actually compose.

### AI & LLM Integration
Bringing large language models into MBSE workflows where they earn their keep — model authoring assistance, validation, summarization, and analysis acceleration. Pragmatic, not magical.

### Simulation & System Analyses
Behavioral and parametric analyses that let your team validate designs against requirements before metal gets cut.
